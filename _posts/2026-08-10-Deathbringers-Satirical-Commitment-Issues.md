Deathbringer is bad satire, it is trying to simultaneously mock and be
80s grimdark chain-mail bikini fantasy. That's why it sucks. Now,
Magnolia Keep[^mk] has definitively killed and then kicked the metaphorical
horse that is "the hottest TTRPG on crowdfunding". I am not
here to just jump on the bandwagon, as fun as that has been on Discord.
See I suffer from the unfortunate disease of being able to see the
potential in things and wanting to fix it. Doubly so when the thing
that is so clearly missing is a sense of humour. Plus I keep getting
ads for this stupid thing so I need to work the sliver out of my brain
somehow.

## The Problem

Everything wrong with this game comes from it trying to have its cake
and eat it too. It clearly and desperately wants to be seen as a satirical
take on 80s chain-mail bikini fantasy whilst also relishing in the male
gaze. These two positions cannot coexist; one will overtake the other
and in this case it has erred on the side of making every woman an
underwear model. No joke you can write can get you past that first
impression.

Now of course there were other issues (outlined by Magnolia Keep[^mk])
but they stem from this root. Every bad, tone deaf choice made comes
from a fundamental lack in being able to commit to the bit. It shows
in the highly rendered 5e style art rather than anything resembling
Frazetta, the ads showing characters in a metal band mimicking 5e ad
tropes and the confused comparisons to Dark Sun despite only really
sharing tone. It reeks of an unclear core vision for the project, instead
being a mishmash of contradicting elements. It's a mess.

## The Solution

The core idea is, I think, a good one. A satirical take on 80s grimdark
chain-mail bikini fantasy is at least somewhat new ground in the OSR[^osr].
All they needed to do was have a writer help Prof DM create a consistent
comedic voice and a lot of the issues would have been avoided. It needs
a clear angle so the other decisions can be filtered through it. If
the Professor had just committed to the satire, all this might
have gone differently.

Now the question is how could this have been more effective satire,
rather than a regressive revel in old tropes? In the spirit of fixing
things I want to answer that question. Not that I think anyone will
listen and change this already very successful if fundamentally flawed
game. If anything it is more working out my own creative muscles, testing
my own comedic chops against a premise I would not have picked for
myself. So without further ado let's start with...

### The Deathbringer Himself

It's clear that the Deathbringer is intended as a comedic character.
Ignoring that he may have been nicked from Games Workshop, he's an
overblown version of the violent barbarian hero. He lives for combat
and taking treasure from the newly deceased. The look here also contrasts
heavily with the vitalism of Conan the prototypical barbarian. This
works to depict him as the dumb brute of bad 80s fantasy rather than
the keen and agile Conan. Alone this is functional parody but he is
also this game's poster-boy so we need to do more. We need jokes.

The simplest and most effective use of a character like this is to use
him like Judge Dredd. Early Judge Dredd comics engaged in this nice bit
of double dipping where the Judges and their violent response to crime
functioned as satire of US police officers, and the criminals they
killed functioned as satire of the negative aspects of US culture. It's
fun, addictive and criminally easy to pull off in small doses, like
when you are advertising a TTRPG crowdfunding campaign 👀.

You want to sell your game on how it cuts through the cruft of other
systems and settings? Have Deathbringer literally cut through them.
The ads for this game would be 1000x better if it was just Deathbringer
dismembering an NPC in the middle of their long backstory or another
hero explaining their class features. Oh, this town has a long and storied
history? Well Deathbringer just tossed the history book at your mayor's
head and is hacking off his limbs. What now?

### Chainmail Bikinis

Every woman on the crowdfunding page is a small-waisted, big chested
white woman. Now I like a sexy lady as much as the next guy but do we
really need a cookie cutter army of them. I'll give you one Red Sonja
parody given the subject matter but then you have to shake it up. In
a world so violent as to make a character like Deathbringer there is
no way *all* the women would be this comfortable being next to nude.
Not only that but they are all the same kind of attractive woman with
nothing for the ass man. Frank Frazetta would be ashamed.

The most obvious solution (other than including some less sexualised
and more diverse women) is to put a dude in chainmail underwear too.
Of course for this to be effective satire it needs to risk upsetting
the exact guys who are into the chainmail bikini. This means making
your male underwear model attractive to women and the male gays.
It's the basic "let's see how you like it" play where the normal people
in the audience get to laugh at everyone who takes it too seriously.

Another way to deal with the sexism of the material we're parodying is
to just plop a modern woman into it. Make a normally dressed straight-woman
to play off the setting's silly or regressive aspects. Better yet have
her tag along with Deathbringer to offset his ultra-violence. Now you
have an audience POV lens to help frame everything as something to
point and laugh at.

### Lady Deathbringer

The gender-swap of our poster-boy deserves her own section, because she
is the most obvious symptom of this game's problem with women. She is
supposed to be Deathbringer's female counterpart but instead she is
another bikini girl but just a little more muscular and wearing a
helmet. It sucks. On its most basic level all they needed to do was
give Deathbringer more female proportions, keeping her just as
cartoonishly muscular and just as armoured, and the job would be done.

If you want to have more fun with it you could do a Ms Pacman thing.
Make Lady Deathbringer 100% identical to Deathbringer just with a little
pink bow and maybe some eyelashes on the helmet. It's absurd
era-appropriate parody. It could also allow you to jump on the trend of giving
the grimdark a girly-pop makeover if you go more overboard with the
look.

### Fungus licking cannibal

This one was pretty shocking to me. I could see how someone might
accidentally take the detour from pulp fantasy into bikini girl world
but this choice was just odd. It's out of place and of course pretty
racist. The only way to salvage this, and this is a tremendous leap,
is to turn it into a pun name. The monster is actually a fungus that
licks cannibals. Otherwise just cut it; it's not really worth saving.

### Bringing it all together

Let's bring this all together into what I think would be a pretty good
trailer for this made up better Deathbringer.

We start with a classic scene; an evil wizard atop a ziggurat, ritual
knife held aloft, seeking to plunge it into the scantily clad woman
tied to the blood altar. The steps of the ziggurat are teeming with dark
creatures of ill-intent. Then we see *him* at the bottom of the stairs,
Deathbringer.

The unholy hordes swarm Deathbringer as he begins to swing his axe. Each
stroke of his mighty axe rips through handfuls of the demonic monstrosities
at a time. Now the wizard finally speaks, "It is too late Deathbringer,
it is as foretold--" and his head leaves his body.

"You saved..." starts the former-sacrifice as Deathbringer strolls past,
ignoring her, "me..." Deathbringer leaps down the other side of the
ziggurat to continue his battle.

## Conclusion

The ads for this game have been incessant, which in a way makes sense,
I'm the target audience. I like beer and pretzels D&D and violence and
yes on occasion boobies. The problem is it is not all done with tongue
in cheek. It doesn't commit to the satire and instead asks to be taken
seriously, or at least as seriously as people take 5e. You fundamentally
disrespect your own material when you don't follow through on your premise
like this. Either the setting is a joke (with town names like Pissburg),
or it is not. It can't be both. That's the lesson in all this; pick a
lane and commit to it.
