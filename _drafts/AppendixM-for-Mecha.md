# Appendix M

The M is for mecha.

There is this bizarre phemomenon I have observed among mecha games;
they don't seem to really be made by lovers of the genre. Every
fantasy game has a pretty apparent set of influences, even if they
are kitchen sink and generic. Mecha RPGs seem to only enjoy the
mechanical design and Evangelion. Where's the appretiation for the
classics (read as "my specific tastes")? Mayhaps I need to define
the appendix M of mecha.

## Real Robot

This is a distinction I rarely see made in mecha games. Usually they
slip into the Eva milieau of splitting the difference between this and
the other super-genre super robot. I don't think there is a lot of value
in muddying this distinction as often as it's done. Real robots have
wear and tear, ammo limits, energy restrictions. The machine is treated
as having real limitations.

### Zeta Gundam

Zeta is what I picked over all the other series in the franchise because
it truly embodies what makes Gundam work. The average episode of Zeta is
1/4 giant robot fights and 3/4 melodrama. This is an important ratio
it means you never get tired of the robot fights and you always know
what's at stake. If Kamille falters, Fa will be alone, Quattro "Not
Char" Bajeena will lose his hope and Bright will have to go back to
hitting his own kids.

The ongoing political drama is also vital for Gundam and the genre as
a whole.
